Wings: buildings that turn at a corner

A corner unit turns the building, so the units no longer sit in one line. They make an L, a U, a C or a longer path. PAD shows one straight run of units at a time (a wing). When the strip is scrolled to its end on a corner unit and there is more building beyond it, the view turns 90 degrees and shows the next wing, still reading left to right.

How wings are made
- The units run in order along the building. A corner unit on the right (windows on its right) ends its wing, and the building turns right. A corner unit on the left starts a wing, and the building turns left.
- Add a unit with the arrow at the end of the strip. Beyond a corner, the new unit starts the next wing and the view turns to it. Earlier, a corner closed its end and nothing could be added past it.
- Group by bedrooms shows the whole building in one strip, so wings are not used while it is on.

Moving between wings
- Scroll to the end of a wing that ends on a corner. After a short pause (about half a second) the view turns by itself. Scrolling back to the start of a later wing turns it back.
- The Turn button at the edge of the screen does the same. It shows at the end of a wing when the strip fits on screen, so nothing needs scrolling. Back to wing N shows at the start of a later wing.
- On a phone, Next at the last unit of a wing turns to the next wing, and Previous at the first unit turns back. There is no automatic turn on a phone, because the strip snaps from unit to unit.
- A tag at the top left says which wing is on screen and which way it heads (the first wing heads east, a right turn heads south, and so on). People who prefer less motion see the change at once, with no swing.

Keyplan
- The keyplan draws every wing, each with its corridor and the units on both sides, turned at the corners. The text under it gives the number of wings and each turn. Efficiency and totals cover the whole building, not only the wing on screen.

Saving
- The order along the building is saved with the building (`order` in the saved data), so wings come back as they were.
